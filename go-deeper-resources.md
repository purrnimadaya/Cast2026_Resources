## Certifications & standards

**ISTQB Certified Tester – AI Testing (CT-AI v2.0)**
The closest thing to an industry standard for testing AI-based systems — covers probabilistic behavior, model drift, and the ISO/IEC 25059 AI quality characteristics.
https://istqb.org/certifications/certified-tester-ai-testing-ct-ai/

**Download 29119-11 - Free Resources**

**OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications**
The reference list for AI-specific risks — prompt injection, hallucination, excessive agency. Free, and widely cited by auditors and regulators.
https://github.com/OWASP/www-project-ai-testing-guide

## Open-source frameworks (hands-on, free)

**DeepEval**
Open-source Python framework that treats LLM evaluations as unit tests. Integrates with pytest and CI/CD — a good Monday-morning starting point.
https://deepeval.com/

**Ragas**
Open-source framework specifically for evaluating RAG and retrieval quality — ties directly to the "data & retrieval quality" dimension of the framework in this talk.
https://github.com/explodinggradients/ragas
